Questions

Can I import books from Kindle, Apple Books, or Audible?
No. Those files are locked by the companies that sold them, and no app can open them — not Aloud, not anything else. Aloud reads DRM-free files you own.
What file types work?
EPUB, PDF, and TXT.
Where does my audio go?
Nowhere. Aloud makes the audio on your phone, and it stays there. There is no account, no server, and nothing to upload.
Does it work without a connection?
Yes. After the first voice download, everything works on a plane, underground, or anywhere with no signal.
A name is pronounced wrong.
Email the book title and the word. Pronunciation fixes are quick to add, and once fixed they stick across the whole series.
Something is broken.
Email what happened and what you expected instead. Your phone model helps.
I paid, then reinstalled. Where did it go?
Purchases live with your Apple ID, not the app. Open Aloud, go to Settings, and choose Restore Purchases.
