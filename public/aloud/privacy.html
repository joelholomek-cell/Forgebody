Privacy

Last updated 10 August 2026

Aloud collects no personal data. There is no account, no login, and no email required to use it.

What stays on your device

Your books, the audio Aloud generates from them, your listening history and statistics, and any voice recordings you make. None of it is transmitted anywhere. It is stored only on your device, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

When Aloud uses the network

Only to download voice models, and to fetch books and cover images from public catalogues:

  • Project Gutenberg
  • LibriVox
  • Open Library

These requests carry no information identifying you, and Aloud never sends your books, your audio, or your reading history anywhere.

Analytics and advertising

None. Aloud contains no analytics tools, no advertising, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

Purchases

Handled entirely by Apple. Aloud never sees your payment details, and receives no personal information from the transaction.

Children

Aloud collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top will change with it.
